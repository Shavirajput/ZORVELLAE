Regimen Inquiries & Protocols

Empirical advice on transitioning formulations, balancing potent actives, and catering to sensitized cutaneous barriers.

We advocate the "Biological Step-Wise Induction" method. Introduce one new active formulation every 5 to 7 days, beginning with the Peptide Gentle Melt Cleanser and Hyaluronic Drops. Once the baseline lipid hydration profile stabilizes, introduce the nocturnal Ceramide & Bio-Peptide Serum on alternate evenings before moving to daily nightly cadence.
We advise against direct concurrent application. Free ionic copper in tripeptide formulations can oxidize pure L-Ascorbic Acid, rendering both actives physiologically inert and potentially inducing brief surface erythema. We suggest scheduling Vitamin C/Niacinamide during the morning phase and Copper Peptides exclusively during the nocturnal restoration phase.
Immediately suspend all chemical exfoliants (AHA/BHA) and high-percentage vitamin serums. Rely exclusively on our "Acute Shield Protocol": Gentle Melt Cleanser without mechanical friction, followed immediately by generous layers of Ceramide Bio-Peptide Serum and Squalane Cream twice daily until stratum corneum cohesion is re-established (typically 7–10 circadian cycles).
Both the Morning Diurnal Defense and Evening Nocturnal Repair routines are free from synthetic retinoids, hydroquinone, and salicylic acid, making them safe for nursing and gestation. We recommend consulting with your obstetrics physician regarding the 10% L-Lactic Reset Peel due to heightened maternal sensitivity.
