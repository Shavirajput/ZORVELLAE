## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the convergence of high-efficacy dermatology and sensorial luxury rituals. Designed for an audience that demands both empirical scientific proof and tactile indulgence, the visual atmosphere is calm, immaculate, and editorial. 

The aesthetic style is **Warm Editorial Minimalism**:
- Generous, intentional negative space evoking a pristine lab environment balanced with the warmth of a restorative wellness spa.
- Refined horizontal and vertical hairline dividers inspired by heritage apothecary labels and high-end monographs.
- Restrained interactions that prioritize effortless discoverability, transparency of scientific ingredient data, and frictionless commerce.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, clinical dialogue:

- **Primary (`#2B1F17` Espresso):** Deep roasted brown used for primary brand typography, wordmarks, primary action buttons, structural borders, and critical UI anchors. Replaces harsh synthetic black with rich organic depth.
- **Secondary (`#D9B8A4` Rose Beige):** Warm, skin-toned neutral applied to secondary cards, sub-navigation panels, and accent borders.
- **Tertiary (`#6B7F6E` Sage Green):** Botanical herbal tone dedicated to active ingredient badges, clinical validation tags, sustainability callouts, and positive success states.
- **Neutral (`#F8F5EF` Ivory):** The warm, breathable canvas base. It softens visual fatigue and provides high contrast for espresso typography without sterile clinical glare.
- **Highlight (`#E9D1CC` Blush):** Soft rose tint reserved for special offers, bundle highlights, seasonal drops, and subtle notification pills.

### Usage Hierarchy
- **60% Ivory Background:** Canvas surfaces, negative space, and modal backgrounds.
- **15% Espresso:** High-contrast text, iconography, primary CTAs, and active strokes.
- **10% Rose Beige:** Alternating container tiers, table rows, and secondary outlines.
- **10% Sage Green:** Botanical proof points, dermatological seals, and active toggles.
- **5% Blush:** Accent badges, limited-run pills, and hover states.

## Typography

Typography delivers a dialogue between classic editorial publishing and contemporary scientific clarity.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the heritage and craftsmanship of haute skincare. Titles should maintain an open line height to avoid glyph collisions and evoke the cadence of luxury print magazines.
- **Interface & Scientific Copy (`Inter`):** Delivers clean optical rhythm, legibility, and technical precision for formulation percentages, ingredient profiles, pricing, and checkout forms.
- **Labels & Micro-headers:** Rendered in `Inter` with uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em` to `0.15em`), mirroring the meticulous typesetting of pharmaceutical packaging.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid designed for editorial compositions:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fixed max width of `1360px` centered within a dynamic viewport. Gutters are `1.5rem` (`24px`) with outer canvas margins of `2rem` (`32px`) to `4rem` (`64px`). Product galleries alternate between symmetric dual columns (6/6) and asymmetric editorial layouts (7/5).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margin boundaries.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Fluid columns with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and margins. Key commerce controls (sticky cart bars, formulation accordions) lock to edge margins.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Generous vertical sections leverage `space-2xl` (`64px`) and `space-3xl` (`96px`) to create breathing room between narrative chapters, clinical evidence modules, and transactional catalogs.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids aggressive, heavy drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering and Low-Contrast Hairline Outlines**:

- **Surface Tiers:** Surfaces sit flat on `#F8F5EF` Ivory. Layering is achieved by shifting nested containers to `#FFFFFF` (for product focal cards) or `#F2ECE1` (for secondary analytical modules).
- **Hairlines:** Structural borders use `#2B1F17` at `10%` to `15%` opacity (`1px` width), creating soft architectural division without visual clutter.
- **Ambient Elevation (Floating Controls):** Modals, quick-view drawers, and sticky purchase bars use an ultra-diffused, warm-tinted shadow: `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(43, 31, 23, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(43, 31, 23, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture relies on **Soft Architectural Geometry (`roundedness: 1`)**.

Corners feature an intentional, subtle curvature (`0.25rem` / `4px` default radius) rather than aggressive bubble rounding. This honors the precise glass-and-dropper packaging of pharmaceutical bottles. Larger surfaces (product hero cards, informational modals) scale up to `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`). Floating botanical tag pills and circular formula swatches utilize fully rounded capsules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Bag, Checkout):** Solid `#2B1F17` Espresso background with `#F8F5EF` Ivory text. Rectangular with soft `4px` radius, uppercase `label-lg` tracking, and `48px` default height. Hover transitions subtly into a deep umber tint (`#3F2E23`).
- **Secondary Action (Discover Formulation, View Ritual):** Transparent background with a `1px` solid `#2B1F17` border (20% opacity default, 100% on hover) and `#2B1F17` text.
- **Tertiary / Botanical (Quick Refill, Sample Add):** Transparent background with `#6B7F6E` text and an animated underline on hover.

### Chips & Botanical Tags
- **Clinical Badges:** Compact capsule or soft pill container filled with `#6B7F6E` (10% tint) and `#6B7F6E` text. Accompanied by minimalist outline line icons (e.g., pipette, leaf, cruelty-free ribbon).
- **Promo / Seasonal Chips:** Filled with `#E9D1CC` Blush with `#2B1F17` text.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Clean borderless ivory containers or framed with `1px` subtle `#D9B8A4` outlines.
- **Imagery:** High-contrast studio macro shots of textures, viscous drops, and amber bottles over neutral off-white backdrops.
- **Meta Hierarchy:** Small uppercase subtext (`label-sm`) for volume and clinical claim, Playfair Display title (`headline-sm`), followed by clean Inter pricing.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Inputs:** Bordered baseline or subtle full border in `#2B1F17` (20% opacity), expanding to a crisp `1px` `#2B1F17` focus ring with no fuzzy glow. Background remains `#FFFFFF` or transparent.
- **Subscription / Variant Selectors:** Radio cards with `1px` border that transitions to solid Espresso stroke when selected, accompanied by a Sage Green savings indicator tag.

### Lists & Formulation Accordions
- **Editorial Accordions:** Full-width rows divided by hairline strokes (`#2B1F17` at 12% opacity). Headline in `Playfair Display` with delicate `+` and `-` glyphs in Inter. Expands smoothly to display active ingredient percentages, clinical trial statistics, and ritual application steps.
