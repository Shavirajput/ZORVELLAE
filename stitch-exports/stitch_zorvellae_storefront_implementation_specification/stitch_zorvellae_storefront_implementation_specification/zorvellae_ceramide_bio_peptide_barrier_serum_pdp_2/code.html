Cellular Architecture

The 3:1:1 Golden Bio-Identical Ratio

The human stratum corneum is composed of lipids precisely arranged in an extracellular matrix. When this matrix is stressed by climate, harsh surfactants, or age, cellular coherence falters.

Zorvellae engineers this barrier serum using the scientifically validated 3:1:1 lipid architecture: three parts bio-fermented ceramides (NP, AP, EOP), one part plant cholesterol, and one part free fatty acids. It doesn't sit on the surface—it integrates effortlessly into your skin’s own structural scaffolding.
