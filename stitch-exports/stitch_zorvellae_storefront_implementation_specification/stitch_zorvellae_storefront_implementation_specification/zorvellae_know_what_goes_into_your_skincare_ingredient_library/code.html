Pharmacological Verification

Ingredient Monograph FAQs

Common clinical questions regarding our extraction standards, purity verifications, and skin tolerance trials.

How does Zorvellae verify the purity and bio-availability of its actives?

expand_more
Every incoming raw batch undergoes High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) and Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR) spectroscopy to certify the exact target molecular mass and ensure zero residual solvents. Only batches verifying >99% active purity are approved for sterile emulsification in our ISO 22716 lab facility.

Why do you avoid essential oils even though they are natural?

expand_more
While naturally derived, essential oils contain high concentrations of volatile terpenes (such as limonene, linalool, and geraniol). When exposed to oxygen on the stratum corneum, these terpenes oxidize into allergenic peroxides that induce sub-clinical inflammation and breakdown of structural collagen fibers over time.

What makes cold-fermented botanical actives superior to conventional extracts?

expand_more
Conventional extraction techniques rely on thermal boiling or synthetic solvent steeping, which denatures delicate polyphenols and phytochemical structures. Our cold-fermentation process utilizes probiotic enzymes to gently break down large botanical molecules into low-Dalton bio-peptides that your skin cells can immediately absorb without barrier friction.

Are all Zorvellae formulations safe for reactive, rosacea-prone, or sensitized skin?

expand_more
Yes. Every formulation undergoes Human Repeat Insult Patch Testing (HRIPT) on sensitized cohorts. By eliminating synthetic fragrances, volatile essential oils, and unbuffered acids, our products minimize neurogenic reactivity while reinforcing the lipid barrier.
